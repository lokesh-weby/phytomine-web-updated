ABOUT US

Phytomine is an innovative research-driven platform focused on sustainable extraction of rare earth elements using natural biological processes. Instead of relying on traditional, environmentally damaging mining techniques, Phytomine leverages the unique ability of certain plants to absorb and accumulate valuable metals from soil, offering an eco-friendly alternative for metal recovery.

The system is built around the concept of phytomining, where hyperaccumulator plants such as ferns are cultivated in metal-rich soils to naturally extract rare earth elements. These plants absorb metals during their growth cycle, transforming contaminated or mineral-rich soil into a renewable resource for metal recovery while minimizing ecological harm.

Phytomine integrates scientific analysis with intelligent data processing to monitor plant growth, metal uptake, extraction efficiency, and environmental safety. By using data-driven evaluation methods, the platform ensures accurate assessment of sustainability indicators such as soil metal reduction, safety index, and environmental impact.
