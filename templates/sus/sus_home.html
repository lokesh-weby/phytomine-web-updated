About Us

The Sustainer Module is designed to ensure environmental stability and long-term ecosystem health after the phytomining and extraction processes. It focuses on maintaining soil quality, land usability, and ecological balance.

This module evaluates post-extraction parameters such as soil nutrient levels, residual metal presence, and land recovery indicators. These assessments help determine whether the site is suitable for continued cultivation or natural restoration.

Using monitored sustainability metrics, the Sustainer Module supports informed decision-making for soil rejuvenation and resource reuse. It ensures that phytomining activities do not compromise future agricultural or ecological potential.
