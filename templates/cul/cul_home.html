About Us

The Cultivator Module is designed to support the critical role of plant growth and management in the phytomining process. It focuses on cultivating hyperaccumulator plants under controlled conditions to ensure healthy growth and optimal metal absorption.

Through this module, cultivators work with validated input parameters provided by the admin, such as biomass limits, growth duration, and soil conditions. These inputs guide cultivation practices and ensure consistency with the overall phytomining strategy

The module enables systematic tracking of fern growth performance, including biomass development, growth rate, and efficiency. By monitoring these indicators, cultivators can ensure that plants reach the required maturity for effective metal accumulation.
