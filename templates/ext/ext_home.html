ABOUT US

The Extractor Module is the core processing unit of the Phytomine system, responsible for converting metal-enriched plant biomass into recoverable metal content. It bridges the gap between biological accumulation and practical metal recovery.

This module operates using scientifically validated extraction parameters provided by earlier stages, ensuring safe, efficient, and controlled metal separation from harvested biomass. The process prioritizes accuracy while maintaining environmental responsibility.

The Extractor Module supports analysis of extraction efficiency by processing biomass data, metal concentration levels, and recovery outputs. These calculations help determine how effectively metals are extracted from plant material.
